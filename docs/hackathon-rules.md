# Hackathon rules and what they mean for Pip

The official rules of the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, as provided by the organisers, with our consequences.

| Rule | Consequence for us |
|---|---|
| **New work only.** Everything presented must be built during the hackathon, 26 Oct – 4 Nov 2026. | All code is written in this repo from 30 Oct onwards. We copy nothing from earlier projects. Third-party open-source libraries are allowed and are listed as such. |
| **Identify your contributions.** The demo shows only features, code and functionality built during the event; judges must be able to tell what is new, or the project is disqualified. | The README gets a "Built during the hackathon" section and a list of third-party components. The commit history is our evidence. The demo shows only Pip's own features. |
| **Public repo.** | `github.com/EVERRONE/MetaHackathon` is public. No secrets in the repo, ever. |
| **Teams.** At most 4 people, solo allowed. One track only; one person submits; every team member is added. | Indie Developer track. Team members are added on the submission. |
| **Demo video.** 3 minutes, required, and one of the main scoring items. | The script follows once the technology works. Every feature needed for the video is in the build plan. |
| **Banned projects.** Projects that break legal, ethical or platform policies, or that use code, data or assets without the rights to them. Also generic projects: AI medical/health advice, basic RAG, simple image analysers, "AI for education" chatbots, job screeners, nutrition coaches, personality analysers, sports analysers or coaches. | Pip is a guided-procedure runtime with grounding and verification, **not** an image analyser or a chatbot. Medical and health tasks are excluded by policy. We do not commit third-party manuals or images we have no rights to: test media are our own photos, and manuals are referenced or used only where the licence allows. |
| **Theme.** Build for Your World (Meta's models in a domain you know, solving a real problem) or Push the Frontier (hard problems in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, science). | Decided together with the story; the technology fits both. |
| **Deadline.** Wed 4 Nov, 21:00 ET (5 Nov, 03:00 CET). Submit at https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit with the public repo, the demo video link, and every team member. | Internal target: submitted by 4 Nov, 18:00 CET. |
| **Waiver.** Participants are 18+ and of legal age; Meta and Cerebral Valley staff, contractors, government-affiliated people and their households are excluded. | — |
