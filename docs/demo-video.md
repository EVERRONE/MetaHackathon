# Demo video: script and shot list (3:00)

The video is one of the main scoring items. It shows only what was built during the hackathon, live
on the iPhone. Pip's answers are not scripted: the wording below is what Pip should show and say,
and it varies from take to take. Retake a scene rather than cut inside an answer.

**Story in one line:** your world is full of things to operate and things you put down somewhere;
Pip points at exactly what to touch, stays on it, and remembers where you left it.

## Before recording

1. Install the build of the day and open Pip once for two minutes (the on-device models load after
   an install). Close it.
2. Pip's memory: remember the objects again with this build, so they keep a reference photo:
   - at the desk, from 40-60 cm with the whole pen and its neighbours in view: "Where is my fancy
     pen?", check the outline is on the right pen, then "Remember this as my fancy pen";
   - the green book, the whole book in view: "Remember this as The Let Them Theory book".
3. If a scene crosses floors, walk once with Pip open from upstairs to downstairs (one session that
   sees both joins them).
4. Lights on as during the takes. Start each take facing furniture, not a mirror, glass or the floor.
5. Screen recording with the microphone on (Control Centre, long press on record, Microphone on), so
   both your voice and Pip's are in the recording. Do-not-disturb on.
6. Nothing personal in view: letters, screens with mail, faces of others.

## If a take goes wrong

- Pip points at the wrong thing: say "No, the other one", keep it if Pip corrects itself (it shows
  the loop), otherwise retake.
- The search takes long (10-20 s): cut the waiting, keep the card appearing and the answer.
- The room is not recognised at once: look round slowly at walls and furniture; Pip asks for it
  itself after 8 s. Cut the waiting if it is long.
- Pip talks over you: tap the microphone button once to stop it.
